# Collaboration Pathway

OBSCURA is open to serious collaboration while the complete implementation
remains private.

## Good collaboration targets

- Independent physics and numerical-method audits
- Full-wave or experimental comparison designs
- Metamaterial constitutive retrieval and calibration
- Quantum-information and channel-distinguishability review
- Robust-control benchmarking and ablation studies
- Scientific visualization and human-factors evaluation
- Reproducibility infrastructure using sanitized artifacts

## What to submit

Open the **Collaboration request** issue template and provide:

1. Your name, affiliation, and professional contact.
2. The precise research question or engineering contribution.
3. A concrete deliverable and expected timeline.
4. Evidence of relevant work, such as papers, repositories, or a portfolio.
5. The minimum private access needed and why public exports are insufficient.
6. Expected attribution, publication, confidentiality, and IP arrangements.

## Access principles

Private access is discretionary, least-privilege, purpose-limited, and revocable.
A successful request may begin with a sanitized dataset or narrow interface
rather than complete source access. Depending on the work, access may require a
confidentiality agreement, contributor agreement, research collaboration
agreement, or institution-to-institution terms.

Do not post confidential proposals, unpublished enabling details, credentials,
or proprietary third-party information in a public issue.
